# early character sketches

## the npc companions

- Sebastian, the Squire
- Serena, the Novice
- Nicole, the Urchin

Sebastian and Serena are twins, son and daughter of a more powerful noble who has sent his children to your protection (in the full story, he would be secretly allied with the resistance against the tyrant king). They are around the same age of the daughter character. coming from the capital, they are naturally arrogant towards the daughter, child of such a minor lord in the remote country. they are the red oni, blue oni trope; Sebastian is a hothead and Serena is the cunning one. both start as rivals (not enemies per se) of the daughter and can be won over to her side (depending on progression).

Nicole is the childhood friend; her narrative arc is instead about reconciling her feelings of inferiority towards the daughter. she and the daughter grew up roving the town's backstreets together, but as the daughter is pulled more towards her noble calling, Nicole has become more aware of the discrepancy between their societal ranks. so her arc is about figuring out what this means for them and coming to support and be supported by the daughter (or not, in the full game; for POC the stories are sort of just a 'ride' with one ending).

## the tutors

- Ser Bedivere, the Hedge Knight
- Diogenes, the Hermit
- Hancock, the Smuggler

I envision Ser Bedivere going from a somewhat comedic figure, to someone the daughter respects. He is past his prime and 'peacocks' a bit, reminiscing on his prowess in earlier years with exaggerated tales. But he is truly an honorable and thoughtful knight, who cares for the daughter (I assume he played a role in rescuing her during the coup).

Diogenes, somewhat like his namesake, is a bit of an unconventional ascetic. he's more of a grandfatherly figure, growing from a curmugeon to trusted mentor. of course he initially clashes with the prim and proper Serena who has been schooled in the dominant church orthodoxy.

Hancock is of course the thief with a heart of gold. perhaps he is a bit of a robin hood figure. he's a younger figure. I'm thinking Han Solo, and the lightning pirates from Stardust.

## The job bosses

- Baldwin, the Guard Captain
- Jerome, the Deacon
- Helga, the Alewife
- Olaf, the Stablehand

Baldwin is straight-laced captain of the town watch. former soldier who takes the defense of his Lord's realm seriously.

Jerome is also a bit straight-laced and a member of the church orthodoxy, which puts him at odds with Diogenes. 

Helga is a kindly younger woman who works at the tavern (she's not the owner, but supervises the daughter as a fellow server).

Olaf is the bumbling stablehand who oversees the daughter's work there.
