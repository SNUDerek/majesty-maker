Here are the six scenes drafted for the Father-Daughter arc, leaning into the reluctant guardian theme and the "blood vs. nurture" emotional core.

As requested, they follow visual novel conventions: narration is explicitly labeled, the Father's inner thoughts are formatted in `(parentheses)`, and each scene features a conditional text block and an "illusion of choice" to give the player agency over the Father's emotional framing.

---

### Scene 1: The Restless Fire (Month 1)

**Narration:** It is well past midnight. You find her in the courtyard, aggressively drilling sword forms by the light of a single lantern.
**[CONDITIONAL: if Fatigue > 50]**

* **Then:** **Narration:** She looks exhausted, bags forming under her eyes, her strikes growing sloppy from sheer exertion.
* **Else:** **Narration:** She is intensely focused, ignoring the late hour and the cold air, driven by an invisible engine.

**Father:** *(She doesn't need to work this hard. The estate will be hers regardless. Why is she doing this?)*
**Father:** [CHOICE]

* **Option A (Strict):** "Go to bed. The estate will not collapse if you take a night off."
* **Option B (Soft):** "Why are you pushing yourself so relentlessly? You have nothing to prove to me."

**Daughter:** I don't know why. I just... I feel like I'm running out of time. Like there's a fire at my back and I have to be ready.
**Father:** *(The Conjunction. She feels the pull of the stars, even if she doesn't know what it means.)*
**Father:** "The only thing you need to be ready for is tomorrow's chores. Put the sword away."

### Scene 2: The Shadows of the Past (Month 3)

**Narration:** Breakfast is completely silent. She is staring blankly at her untouched bowl of porridge.
**[CONDITIONAL: if Melancholy > 40]**

* **Then:** **Daughter:** "I had the nightmare again. The fire, the castle... the woman crying."
* **Else:** **Daughter:** "I had a strange dream last night. A castle burning. A man in black armor."

**Father:** *(Vortigan. And Queen Isolde. The memories of the coup are waking up.)*
**Father:** [CHOICE]

* **Option A (Dismissive):** "It is just the stress of your studies. Eat your breakfast."
* *(If I validate it, she will pull at the thread. I must keep her anchored here.)*


* **Option B (Comforting):** "Dreams can be frightening, but you are safe here with me."
* *(I promised the Queen I would protect her. The truth will only paint a target on her back.)*



**Daughter:** "It didn't feel like a dream, Father. It felt like a memory I'm not supposed to have."
**Father:** "You have a vivid imagination. Finish your food, your tutors are waiting."

### Scene 3: The Question of Purpose (Month 5)

**Narration:** She is packing her satchel for the day, pausing to look at her heavy, unusual curriculum.
**[CONDITIONAL: if Oratores > 30]**

* **Then:** **Daughter:** "Diogenes is teaching me courtly Latin and the laws of royal succession."
* **Else:** **Daughter:** "Ser Bedivere is drilling me on heavy cavalry formations and siege tactics."

**Daughter:** "Why? I'm the daughter of a borderland knight. I'm never going to command an army or parley with the Pope."
**Father:** *(The tutors know. Or they suspect. They're quietly preparing her for a war I don't want her to fight.)*
**Father:** [CHOICE]

* **Option A (Deflective):** "A well-rounded mind is a sharp weapon. Do not question your elders."
* **Option B (Pragmatic):** "The world is wider and more dangerous than this estate. You must be prepared for anything."

**Daughter:** "It feels like you're all preparing me for a life that isn't mine."
**Father:** *(It is exactly your life. I am just terrified for you to live it.)*

### Scene 4: The Revelation (Month 7)

**Narration:** You enter your study. She is standing by your open lockbox, holding a heavy gold signet ring bearing the royal crest.
**[CONDITIONAL: if Laboratores > 40]**

* **Then:** **Narration:** You hid the key well, but she picked the lock with the skills Hancock taught her.
* **Else:** **Narration:** You left the lockbox open in your haste this morning. A foolish, irreversible mistake.

**Daughter:** "This is King Cornelius's seal. And this baby blanket... it has the royal crest. Who am I?"
**Father:** *(The day I have dreaded for fourteen years is here.)* "You are the true heir to the throne. You are the Queen's daughter."
**Daughter:** "Everything... my whole life was a lie? Just to hide me?"
**Father:** [CHOICE]

* **Option A (Justify):** "It was the only way to keep you alive! Vortigan killed everyone else."
* **Option B (Apologize):** "I am so sorry. I promised your mother I would protect you, whatever the cost."

**Daughter:** "Protect me? You made me a ghost!"
**Narration:** She drops the heavy gold ring onto the desk and flees the room.

### Scene 5: The 'Man of Steel' Moment (Month 9)

**Narration:** The rain outside is deafening. She sits by the hearth, her knees pulled to her chest, looking younger than she has in years.
**[CONDITIONAL: if Melancholy > 60]**

* **Then:** **Narration:** The weight of the kingdom is crushing her. She is shivering, though the fire is warm.
* **Else:** **Narration:** The anger has faded, leaving only a quiet, terrifying uncertainty in her eyes.

**Daughter:** "I don't want it. The crown, the prophecy, the blood. I don't want to lead an army."
**Daughter:** "Can't I just... can't I just keep pretending I'm your daughter?"
**Father:** *(My heart breaks for her. I wish to the saints I could say yes.)*
**Father:** [CHOICE]

* **Option A (Duty):** "The realm is bleeding. It needs its Queen. But I will always be your father."
* **Option B (Love):** "You *are* my daughter. A crown will never change that."

**Daughter:** "Are you sure? Even when they find out I'm a fraud? That I'm just a country girl?"
**Father:** "I raised you. I watched you grow. Blood does not make a father, and blood does not make a Queen. Your choices do."

---

### Scene 6: The 'Dune' Departure (Month 11)

**Narration:** The eve of the Pilgrimage. Her pack is sealed. The heavy Pilgrim's cloak rests on her shoulders.
**[CONDITIONAL: if Bellatores + Oratores + Laboratores > 150]**

* **Then:** **Narration:** She stands tall, a formidable presence. A true Sovereign, ready to forge her own destiny.
* **Else:** **Narration:** She still looks so small wrapped in that heavy cloak, stepping into an impossibly large world.

**Daughter:** "The Conjunction is tomorrow. I know what I have to do now."
**Father:** *(I cannot protect her by hiding her anymore. I can only protect her by trusting the woman I raised.)*
**Father:** [CHOICE]

* **Option A (Practical):** "Trust your training, and trust the allies you've made. They will follow you."
* **Option B (Emotional):** "Whatever kind of Queen you choose to be... I know it will be the right one."

**Daughter:** "I will come back for you. When the Usurper falls."
**Father:** "I know you will. May the saints guide you... my daughter."
